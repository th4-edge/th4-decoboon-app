## 后端修复

补充套餐模板时触发后端真实异常：

```text
A collection with orphan deletion was no longer referenced by the owning entity instance:
PackageTemplate.items
```

修复 `PackageConfigContext.recalculate`，不再替换 Hibernate 托管集合，改用 `clear/addAll`。修复后 `pack/line/save` 成功，模板成本重算为 `27.40`。
